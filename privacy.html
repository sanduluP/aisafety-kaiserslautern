Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. When you open the site, your browser necessarily transmits technical data to the hosting infrastructure, which may be processed in short‑lived logs. This typically includes:

  • your IP address,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the page requested,
  • the referring page, if any,
  • your browser and operating system.

This processing is technically necessary to deliver the website and to protect it from abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functioning, secure website). We do not combine this data with other sources and do not use it to identify individuals. Cloudflare may process data outside the EU on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. If you use the light/dark theme switch, your choice is saved in your own browser’s local storage under the key theme. That value never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Contacting us

If you email us, we process the content of your message and your address in order to reply, on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR. We keep such correspondence only as long as it is needed, and do not pass it on to third parties.

External links

This site links to external websites such as safeaigermany.org, aisafety.info, and others. Once you follow such a link, this policy no longer applies and the privacy policy of the site you land on takes over. We have no control over what those sites collect.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • request information about the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have data erased (Art. 17),
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77). For Rhineland‑Palatinate this is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland‑Pfalz.

To exercise any of these, just email us at the address above.
